Refund & Escrow Dispute Policy

Last updated: October 2026 | Nationwide Protection across Zambia

1. The Escrow Hold Period

When you purchase items on Yumilo Market using mobile money (MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money), your payment is not paid directly to the vendor. Instead, funds are securely held in Yumilo Market's escrow holding account until the delivery is successfully completed and verified.

2. Buyer Inspection & Dispute Window

Upon receiving your order delivered via our courier partner network across any of Zambia's 10 provinces, you have a 24-hour inspection window to inspect the goods.

  • Confirmation: If you confirm receipt and item quality, escrow funds are released to the vendor.
  • Dispute: If the item is defective, counterfeit, or significantly not as described, you must file a dispute within 24 hours directly through your Yumilo Market dashboard or by contacting support.

3. Eligible Refund Scenarios

Refunds from escrow are approved under the following conditions:

  • Non-Delivery: The vendor or courier fails to deliver the merchandise within the agreed timeframe and tracking shows no transit progress.
  • Item Defect or Damage: Goods arrive damaged or non-functional, supported by photographic or video evidence submitted within the 24-hour window.
  • Wrong Item: The delivered product does not match the specifications listed on the marketplace catalog.

4. Refund Processing & Mobile Money Reversals

Once a dispute is reviewed and approved by Yumilo Market's mediation team, refunds are processed directly back to the buyer's mobile money wallet (MTN or Airtel) within 3 to 5 business days.

5. Support & Claims Assistance

For urgent return requests or dispute mediation, reach out to our support team at support@yumilomarket.store with your Order ID and transaction reference.
