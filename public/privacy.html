Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 | Operating across all 10 Provinces of Zambia

1. Introduction

Yumilo Market ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you visit our website www.yumilomarket.store or use our escrow marketplace services.

2. Information We Collect

To provide secure nationwide e-commerce and mobile money escrow services, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Personal Identifiers: Full name, phone number, email address, and delivery addresses across any of Zambia's 10 provinces.
  • Vendor & Courier KYC: Business registration documents, NRC or passport details, and tax identification where applicable for verified sellers.
  • Transaction & Payment Data: Mobile money transaction reference IDs (MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money) and order histories. Note: We do not store your direct PINs or private security keys.
  • Technical Data: IP addresses, browser types, device information, and site interaction logs secured via Supabase database infrastructure.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is strictly utilized to:

  • Facilitate secure escrow transactions and verify mobile money payments.
  • Coordinate local and inter-provincial deliveries with our courier partner network.
  • Send automated order updates, verification codes, and WhatsApp alerts.
  • Prevent fraud, resolve disputes, and maintain platform integrity.

4. Data Security & Storage

We implement enterprise-grade security measures, including encrypted protocols (HTTPS/SSL) and secure database partitioning via Supabase, to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Contact & Privacy Inquiries

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to request data correction or deletion, please contact our Zambia Operations Hub at support@yumilomarket.store.
