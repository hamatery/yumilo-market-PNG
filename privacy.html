Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information necessary to facilitate nationwide commerce in Zambia, including your name, delivery address, phone number, and payment verification records.

All user accounts, profiles, and transactional data are secured via encrypted databases with Row Level Security (RLS) policies. Private documents and payment receipts are restricted to authorized administrators only.
