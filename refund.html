Refund & Dispute Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Escrow Protection & Claim Window

Because Yumilo Market operates a secure order review and manual verification process, funds remain secured until delivery is confirmed. Refund claims must be submitted within 48 hours of delivery.

2. Eligible Refund Grounds

Refunds or replacements are evaluated for items arriving damaged, spoiled (in the case of poultry/food products), or significantly not as described in the vendor listing.

3. Dispute Resolution

Escalated refund claims are reviewed by Yumilo Market managers. Approved refunds are processed manually via mobile money or bank transfer within 3 to 5 business days.
